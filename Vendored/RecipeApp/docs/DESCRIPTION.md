# Product Description

The original product description, carried over as Markdown. The app's name is still open; "Recipe app" is the working title.

The Recipe app is a personal recipe app for iPhone and Mac that helps people collect, organize, cook, and share their favorite recipes.

Its goals are to make it easy to import recipes from websites, with an automatic browser fallback, scan printed recipes, add multiple photos, merge duplicates, and organize recipes with ratings and customizable multi-select categories. Recipes should have clear, numbered directions and ingredient lists that can be exported to Apple Reminders groceries.

The app offers Simple and Cookbook appearances, combining an iPhone-friendly interface with a warm cookbook style. Sharing supports contacts and other users of the app. Cross-device sync is a future goal, with backups available during initial testing.
