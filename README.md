# Geist2

A recipe manager for iPhone, iPad and Mac, under construction (working title; the product name comes later). It keeps your recipes, scales them, turns them into shopping lists, walks you through cooking, and plans the week's meals. Everything works offline and stays on your devices.

- Import recipes from websites, the share sheet, pasted text, photos and printed pages (OCR).
- Ingredients are structured (amount, unit, item, note), so recipes scale and convert between US and metric units.
- Shopping lists combine quantities across recipes and can go to Reminders.
- Cook mode, timers, a pantry with "what can I cook", and a meal planner.
- Prep and shopping tasks can go to zDocket.
- Recipe collections are signed, open module files anyone can share.
