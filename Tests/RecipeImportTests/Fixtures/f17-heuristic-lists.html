Weeknight Curry

Serves 4. Prep 15 minutes, cook 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp curry paste
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 lb chicken thighs, cubed
  • 2 cups jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Bloom the paste in oil.
  2. Add chicken and brown.
  3. Pour in coconut milk; simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Serve over rice.
