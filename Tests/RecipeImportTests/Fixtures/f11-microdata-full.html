Root Vegetable Stew

A slow stew of roots and beans.

6 servings Stew American stew, roots, vegan
Example Cook
20 minutes 1 hour 1 hour 20 minutes
  • 3 parsnips, peeled
  • 2 cans white beans
  • 1 bunch kale
  1. Brown the parsnips.
  2. Add beans and stock.
  3. Wilt the kale at the end.
